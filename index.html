Amelie Tolvin

PhD Candidate, Political Science, University of Toronto

I am a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of Toronto and a part of the University of Helsinki's Mannerheim Professorship of Russian Security Studies Working Group.

I study why security services use violence against civilians, with a focus on Russia and Belarus. My doctoral dissertation examines variation in violence against civilians during Russia's invasion of Ukraine; more broadly, I study authoritarian regimes, militaries, and coercive institutions.
