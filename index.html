Amelie Tolvin

PhD Candidate, Political Science

I am a PhD Candidate in Political Science at the University of Toronto and a researcher in the Mannerheim Professorship of Russian Security Studies Working Group at the University of Helsinki.

I study security service violence, with a focus on Russia and Belarus. My doctoral dissertation examines variation in violence against civilians during Russia's invasion of Ukraine; more broadly, my research interests include authoritarian regimes and coercive institutions.
